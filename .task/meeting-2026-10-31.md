# Meeting with SKELAR, 31.10.2026 — key points (summary of the call)

- Each client product has its own rules and gates; Defuse must plug into the client's backend and let the product decide. Example: under ~$50 a full refund can be automatic; bigger sums — human, because the cost of a mistake is higher.
- Human must remain the final instance for risky money decisions. Give the AI full autonomy only where an error is cheap (answers, plan changes, simple actions).
- Agent first decides: request type + probability of its own error; tiers by error rate and by cost of error; above the threshold it stops and asks a human.
- Ideal flow: the agent does ~95% of the work (checks user, plan, payment) and asks the human for a yes/no.
- Analytics is valuable for B2B but a single panel is easy to copy: show segmentation, top-5 request reasons, AI-resolved vs human-assisted, where the AI failed and why (to improve it).
- Build it on the web (all support admin tools are web).
- Pick a concrete domain (e.g. subscription apps), describe the ideal customer and their hard problems that today only humans solve.
