# Analytics — impact, root causes, eval

Measures what Defuse changed (auto-resolved share, money at risk and expected loss avoided, sarcasm and injections caught, time to resolution) against a human-only baseline month, shows why customers write (root causes with a product fix) and reports the eval.

**Who uses it:** support agents, support lead, product team.

**Main metric:** Expected fees avoided ($), auto-resolved %, top root cause share.

**Status:** MVP (hackathon, 31.10.2026).
